# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Niño, primarily while eating out or just after. Context is one-handed, possibly distracted, outdoors or in a restaurant. The job is dual: capture an honest restaurant verdict before it fades, and snap a food photo with background removed for the personal log and juicecolored.com feed. Not a social app; no audience beyond self.

## Product Purpose

JuiceColored Dash is a personal companion for logging where you ate and how you felt about it, plus cutout food photos that sync to the JuiceColored ecosystem. Success is frictionless capture in the moment and a useful personal archive when deciding where to eat again. The web portfolio may surface recent food as a human detail; the app itself is a utility, not a showcase.

## Brand Personality

Utilitarian, fast, native. Personality lives on juicecolored.com; the app clears the way for capture. Apple Notes calm: large titles, continuous corners, warm neutrals, one accent used sparingly. Direct copy ("Save verdict", "Quick capture") over marketing tone.

## Anti-references

- Yelp, Beli, Google Maps review UI: stars, crowdsourced social, ranking theatrics
- Calorie trackers and diet apps: macro counts, guilt framing, health-dashboard chrome
- Dark SaaS dashboards, purple gradients, generic productivity UI
- Generic AI portfolio patterns from the web brand (glass cards, gradient text, hero metrics) if they leak into tool surfaces

## Design Principles

1. **Capture before polish.** Optimize for one-handed, in-the-moment entry; defer decoration and social framing.
2. **Verdict and photo are paired rituals.** Reviews and food cutouts are equal citizens; neither is the "main" product buried in settings.
3. **Native calm over brand theater.** Borrow iOS structure (large titles, continuous curves, field surfaces); save expressive personality for the website.
4. **Honest language, no platform voice.** Say "verdict" and "place", not stars, likes, or gamified badges.
5. **Fix obvious barriers only.** Personal tool: ship usable defaults; address contrast and labels when touching a screen, without a formal audit mandate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Minimal for now: fix obvious barriers when working on a screen (labels on icon buttons, disabled states, selectable text where useful). Honor system dark mode. No formal WCAG audit requirement yet.
